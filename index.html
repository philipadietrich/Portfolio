About Me

I studied mechanical engineering with a specialization in energy systems and environmental engineering. My relevant coursework included SolidWorks, finite element analysis (FEA), fluid mechanics, heat transfer, thermodynamics, statics, and materials. I also recently earned my Certified SOLIDWORKS Associate (CSWA) certification.

My interest in renewable energy comes from a lifelong love of the environment and conservation. Growing up, I watched Shark Week and Animal Planet with my mom, as well as Planet Earth narrated by David Attenborough. That curiosity and passion has stayed with me, which is why I want to use my engineering skills to help make a difference.

FEA Turbofan Simulation Project

For this project, two classmates and I used finite element analysis (FEA) to evaluate a turbofan assembly using geometry from an open-source model. We explored how the assembly behaved under different conditions and used our results to identify important design considerations. It was a really cool opportunity to apply what we’d learned to a complex mechanical system.

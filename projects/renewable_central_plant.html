Renewable Central Plant Replacement Analysis

Renewable Central Plant Analysis

This project analyzed renewable energy systems capable of replacing a university central power plant through large scale energy generation alternatives.

Project Overview

The study compared solar photovoltaic systems, solar thermal systems, wind turbines, and fuel cell based solutions. The objective was to estimate the scale, feasibility, and physical requirements of each approach.

My Role

I performed engineering calculations involving power generation, land area, equipment count, and system feasibility. I also compared tradeoffs between different renewable technologies.

Skills and Tools Used

MATLAB, hand calculations, renewable energy analysis, technical reporting, and engineering comparison methods.

What I Learned

This project improved my ability to evaluate large scale energy systems and communicate technical results in a way that supports engineering decision making.
